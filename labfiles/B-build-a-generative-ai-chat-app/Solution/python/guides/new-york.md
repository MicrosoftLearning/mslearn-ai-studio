# Wingtip Journeys — New York

*Small-group departures, 4 days / 3 nights. Fictitious content created for a training exercise.*

## About this journey

The **Uptown Downtown** trip is Wingtip Journeys' most popular North American departure. It's
a compact long weekend for travelers who want a first, confident taste of the city — capped at
14 travelers, led by a guide who lives in Brooklyn and will tell you so.

## Where you stay

- **The Garment House**, Midtown — a small hotel on West 39th Street, chosen for its walking
  distance to Penn Station and the theatre district.
- **Vinegar Hill Rooms**, Brooklyn — a quieter option across the river for travelers who'd
  rather commute in than sleep in Midtown.

Both properties are included in the trip price. Wingtip Journeys does not arrange
accommodation outside its partner list.

## What's included

- Three nights' accommodation with breakfast
- A guided Lower Manhattan walk: Battery Park, the 9/11 Memorial, and Tribeca
- Timed entry to one major museum of your choice, booked in advance
- A guided evening walk across the Brooklyn Bridge, ending with skyline views from Dumbo
- A seven-day unlimited transit card
- Airport transfers on arrival and departure days

## What's not included

Flights, travel insurance, theatre tickets, meals other than breakfast, and observation deck
admissions.

## Good to know

- **Best months:** late April to early June, and September to early November. July and August
  are hot and humid; January is genuinely cold.
- **Packing:** comfortable walking shoes above everything else. Expect 15,000 steps a day.
- **Getting around:** the subway runs 24 hours and is the fastest way across the city. Your
  guide runs a short "how to read the subway map" session on day one.
- **Theatre:** Wingtip Journeys can advise on which shows are running, but travelers book
  their own tickets.

## Departures and pricing

Departures run weekly year-round. Trips are priced per person sharing a twin room; a single
supplement applies. Contact a Wingtip Journeys consultant for current availability.

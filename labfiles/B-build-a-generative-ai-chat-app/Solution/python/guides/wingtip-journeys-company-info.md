# Wingtip Journeys — Company information

*Fictitious content created for a training exercise. Wingtip Journeys is not a real company.*

## Who we are

Wingtip Journeys is a small-group travel company. We run capped-size guided trips — usually 10
to 14 travelers — led by guides who live in the places they show you. We've been operating
since 2011 and currently run journeys in twelve destinations.

## What we do and don't do

**We do**: design and operate the itinerary, book the accommodation from our partner list,
arrange included activities and entries, provide a local guide throughout, and handle airport
transfers on arrival and departure days.

**We don't**: sell flights, sell travel insurance, or book restaurants, rental cars, or
accommodation outside our partner properties. Our travel consultants will happily advise on all
of these, but you book them yourself.

## Booking and payment

- A deposit secures a place; the balance is due 60 days before departure.
- Bookings made within 60 days of departure are payable in full.
- Trips are priced per person sharing a twin room. A single supplement applies to solo
  travelers who want their own room.

## Changes and cancellations

- Changes requested more than 60 days before departure carry an administration fee only.
- Cancellations within 60 days are subject to a sliding scale published in your booking
  confirmation.
- Wingtip Journeys may cancel a departure that doesn't reach its minimum group size, in which
  case you're offered an alternative date or a full refund.

## Travel insurance

Travel insurance is a condition of travel on every Wingtip Journeys trip. We don't sell it, but
we won't let you board without it.

## Accessibility

Most journeys have step-free or lower-intensity alternatives for at least one day's activity.
Tell your consultant what you need at the time of booking so we can confirm what's possible on
your chosen departure.

## Contact

Travel consultants are available Monday to Saturday. Booked travelers get a 24-hour emergency
number in their final documents, which is answered by a real person in the destination.

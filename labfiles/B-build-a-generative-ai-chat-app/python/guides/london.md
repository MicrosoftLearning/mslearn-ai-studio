# Wingtip Journeys — London

*Small-group departures, 5 days / 4 nights. Fictitious content created for a training exercise.*

## About this journey

**Thames and Thereabouts** is Wingtip Journeys' flagship European city trip: five days built
around walking, museums, and one day out of the city. Capped at 12 travelers.

## Where you stay

- **Bloomsbury Yard**, Bloomsbury — a townhouse hotel a few minutes from the British Museum
  and three Underground lines.
- **Southwark Wharf**, Bankside — riverside, modern, and a short walk from Borough Market.

Both properties are included in the trip price. Wingtip Journeys does not arrange
accommodation outside its partner list.

## What's included

- Four nights' accommodation with breakfast
- A guided Westminster walk: the Houses of Parliament, Whitehall, and St James's Park
- Pre-booked entry to the Tower of London
- A guided day trip to Oxford by train, including a college visit
- A seven-day travel card for zones 1-2
- Airport transfers on arrival and departure days

## What's not included

Flights, travel insurance, meals other than breakfast, and theatre or concert tickets.

## Good to know

- **Best months:** May, June, and September. Expect rain in any month and pack accordingly.
- **Packing:** an umbrella you don't mind losing, and shoes that handle wet pavement.
- **Getting around:** the Underground is included; contactless payment works on buses and
  trains if you'd rather not use the card.
- **Museums:** most national museums have free general admission, which is why the trip
  deliberately leaves two afternoons open.

## Departures and pricing

Departures run weekly from March to October and fortnightly in winter. Trips are priced per
person sharing a twin room; a single supplement applies. Contact a Wingtip Journeys consultant
for current availability.

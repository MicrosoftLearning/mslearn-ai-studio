# Wingtip Journeys — San Francisco

*Small-group departures, 5 days / 4 nights. Fictitious content created for a training exercise.*

## About this journey

Wingtip Journeys runs the **Bay Lights** small-group trip to San Francisco, capped at 12
travelers and led by a local guide. It's built for first-time visitors who want the landmarks
without the coach-tour feeling, and it leaves plenty of afternoons unscheduled.

## Where you stay

Wingtip Journeys books travelers into two partner properties:

- **The Cable House**, Nob Hill — a restored 1920s building three blocks from the cable car
  turnaround. Rooms are compact; the rooftop deck is not.
- **Presidio Gate Lodge**, Marina District — quieter, greener, and a fifteen-minute walk from
  the Palace of Fine Arts. Recommended for travelers bringing children.

Both properties are included in the trip price. Wingtip Journeys does not arrange
accommodation outside its partner list.

## What's included

- Four nights' accommodation with breakfast
- A guided walking day covering Chinatown, North Beach, and the Embarcadero
- A morning ferry to Alcatraz Island with pre-booked audio tour
- A half-day cycle across the Golden Gate Bridge to Sausalito, returning by ferry
- Airport transfers on arrival and departure days

## What's not included

International or domestic flights, travel insurance, lunches and dinners, and anything you buy
on your two free afternoons.

## Good to know

- **Best months:** September and October are the warmest and clearest. Summer mornings are
  often fogged in until midday.
- **Packing:** layers, always. A windproof jacket matters more than a warm one.
- **Getting around:** the trip includes a transit card loaded for the week. Cable cars are
  charming and slow; the Muni Metro is neither.
- **Accessibility:** the Sausalito cycle day has a step-free alternative — a guided ferry and
  waterfront walk. Tell your Wingtip Journeys consultant when you book.

## Departures and pricing

Departures run twice monthly from March to November. Trips are priced per person sharing a
twin room; a single supplement applies. Contact a Wingtip Journeys consultant for current
availability.

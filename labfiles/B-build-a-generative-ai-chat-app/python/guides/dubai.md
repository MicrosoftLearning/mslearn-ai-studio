# Wingtip Journeys — Dubai

*Small-group departures, 5 days / 4 nights. Fictitious content created for a training exercise.*

## About this journey

**Sand and Skyline** pairs two days of modern Dubai with two days in the desert. Capped at 10
travelers, because the desert camp can't take more.

## Where you stay

- **Marina Court**, Dubai Marina — high floors, harbor views, and a tram stop at the door.
- **Al Rimal Camp**, Al Marmoom — a fixed desert camp with permanent en-suite tents, used for
  one night of the trip.

Both properties are included in the trip price. Wingtip Journeys does not arrange
accommodation outside its partner list.

## What's included

- Four nights' accommodation (three in the Marina, one in the desert camp) with breakfast
- A guided old-city walk through Al Fahidi, the textile souk, and an abra crossing of the creek
- Timed entry to the observation deck at the Burj Khalifa
- A guided desert night: dune drive, stargazing, and dinner at camp
- Airport transfers on arrival and departure days

## What's not included

Flights, travel insurance, meals other than breakfast and the camp dinner, and optional
activities such as sandboarding or a hot-air balloon flight.

## Good to know

- **Best months:** November to March. Summer daytime temperatures regularly exceed 40°C and
  the desert night is cancelled between June and August.
- **Packing:** loose, light, covering clothing; a warm layer for the desert after dark.
- **Dress and conduct:** shoulders and knees covered in malls, mosques, and older
  neighborhoods. Your guide briefs the group on arrival.
- **Getting around:** the Metro and tram cover the Marina and Downtown well; the desert leg is
  by private vehicle.

## Departures and pricing

Departures run weekly from October to April. Trips are priced per person sharing a twin room;
a single supplement applies. Contact a Wingtip Journeys consultant for current availability.
